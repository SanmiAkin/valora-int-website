Privacy notice

How we handle your personal information

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Valora International Ltd, a private company limited by shares, registered in England and Wales with company number 17495881. Our registered office is 32 Argyle Square, Ashbrooke, Sunderland SR2 7BS, United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice.

For any question about this notice or your information, contact us through the enquiry form on this website or write to us at our registered office.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, organisation, organisation type and the message you write.
  • Correspondence. If you write to us, or we correspond by email, we keep that correspondence and the contact details in it.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider records basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when the site is visited, for security and to keep the service running.

Please do not send us sensitive personal information through the contact form.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to discuss working together. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and, where you are asking about our services, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To keep the website secure and working. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in operating a safe website.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists unless you have asked us to.

Who we share it with

  • Web3Forms, which delivers contact form messages to our inbox and briefly keeps a copy of each submission.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.
  • Our email provider, which stores the messages we receive and send.
  • Professional advisers or authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens, we rely on recognised safeguards, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK's approved data transfer agreements.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and related correspondence for up to 24 months after our last contact with you. If you become a client, we keep your information for as long as the relationship lasts and for the period the law requires afterwards.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Our fonts are served from our own website, not from a third party.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice if the way we handle personal information changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
